# Proven examples (v2, textured stroke)

These exact prompts produced the "v2" set shown to the owner on 26 November
2026 (Figma `generate_image`, model `gpt-image-2.5-flare`, 1024 × 1024). All
six came back with solid white backgrounds and no stray lettering. Start from
the closest one and change as little as possible.

Every prompt here carries the textured HAND-DRAWN STROKE block and ends with
`opaque white paper background`.

```text

HAND-DRAWN STROKE:
drawn by hand with a real brush pen on paper: confident calligraphic outlines that swell and taper, visible dry-brush texture with broken bristle marks along the edges and at the ends of strokes, slightly uneven ink inside the thick lines and black shapes so a little paper shows through, a gentle natural wobble, a few lines left open or overlapping at the corners, restrained and elegant rather than messy, very simple cartoon face, minimal interior detail, no fine hair strands, no cross-hatching, no scribbling, no drawn surface textures on objects

SHOT VARIABLE:
standing behind a kitchen counter cooking, torso facing the viewer straight on and centred in the picture, framed from the thighs up, stirring a steaming kadai (a deep round-bottomed Indian cooking pan with two small loop handles) on a small gas stove with a flat ladle in one hand, the other hand holding a loop handle with a folded cloth, beside the stove a round masala dabba spice box and a round rolling board with a rolling pin and two rotis, all drawn simply with few lines, wearing a plain white bib apron over a solid black kurta with elbow-length sleeves, apron strings tied at the waist, nothing overlaps the apron bib

EMBLEM:
exactly in the centre of the apron bib, level and horizontally centred, one small emblem drawn in the same brush-pen hand: a horizontal pill-shaped capsule badge about twice as wide as it is tall, filled solid black, with a single smooth white wavy line running across its middle that has exactly two rounded humps like a soft lowercase handwritten m; clear white apron all around the emblem; nothing else on the apron, no pocket, no pattern

cheerful focused expression, a few steam curls and short motion ticks above the kadai, generous white space, opaque white paper background, no text, no lettering, no other logos, no extra characters
```

The emblem came back centred and black, with the right pill proportions and
the two-hump wave. Brush texture shows in the kurta, the kadai and the hair.

## What was rejected before this look

- A cook who did not look Indian.
- A real vector logo pasted onto the apron, off-centre, and in the brand
  colour. The owner wants the emblem centred, black, and drawn in the same hand.
- Tidy, detailed line art with a realistic face.

# Narrative Structures for SaaS Motion Pieces

## Structure 1: The Classic Problem-Solution (30-60s)

The workhorse. Works for feature launches, product explainers, campaign hero videos.

```
HOOK (0-3s)
A striking visual or provocative question that stops the scroll.
Must work with sound OFF (text or visual-only hook).

PROBLEM WORLD (3-15s)
Establish the pain. Make it visceral, relatable, slightly exaggerated.
Build empathy. The audience should think "that's me."
Escalate — the problem compounds, multiplies, gets worse.

THE BREAK (15-18s)
A pause. Silence. Visual reset. The moment before relief.
This is the fulcrum — the most important transition in the piece.

SOLUTION REVEAL (18-30s)
The product/feature enters — not as a screenshot, as a transformation.
The problem world morphs, dissolves, or is replaced.
Show the EFFECT of the solution, not the solution itself.

PAYOFF (30-40s)
The new world. Calm, clear, empowering.
One concrete, specific detail that makes it believable.

CTA + LOGO (40-45s)
Clear action. Logo resolve. Tagline if earned.
```

## Structure 2: The Cold Open (15-30s, social/ads)

Short-form. Designed for social feeds. No preamble.

```
SHOCK/DELIGHT (0-2s)
Start mid-action. No logos, no titles. Pure visual impact.
The audience should stop scrolling before they know what this is.

RAPID ESCALATION (2-8s)
Build intensity fast. Quick cuts or continuous transformation.
Every second must add information or emotion.

TWIST/REVEAL (8-12s)
The product or concept snaps into focus.
"Oh, THAT'S what this is about."

STICKY ENDING (12-15s)
Logo, tagline, URL. But make the final frame iconic —
something that works as a still image / thumbnail.
```

## Structure 3: The Journey (60-90s)

For brand films, annual launches, or high-stakes campaigns.

```
THE WORLD BEFORE (0-20s)
Rich world-building. Establish a character or archetype.
Show their daily reality with warmth and detail.
Subtle hints of friction — things that aren't quite right.

THE INCITING INCIDENT (20-30s)
The problem crystallizes. A moment where the friction becomes undeniable.
The character realizes something has to change.

THE DISCOVERY (30-45s)
The product/feature enters the story naturally.
Not a sales pitch — a genuine discovery moment.
Show the character's reaction: relief, surprise, delight.

THE TRANSFORMATION (45-65s)
The world transforms around the character.
Before/after woven together — not sequential, simultaneous.
The old pain points dissolve one by one.

THE NEW NORMAL (65-80s)
The character in their new reality. Confident, efficient, empowered.
A callback to the opening — same setting, different feeling.

CLOSE (80-90s)
Emotional resolution. Logo. CTA that feels earned, not forced.
```

## Structure 4: The Domino (30-45s)

For interconnected features or platform stories. One action triggers a chain.

```
THE FIRST DOMINO (0-5s)
A single, simple action. A click, a drag, a decision.

THE CHAIN REACTION (5-30s)
Each domino reveals a new capability or transformation.
The chain accelerates — each step faster and more impressive.
Visual connections between steps are explicit and satisfying.

THE RESULT (30-40s)
Pull back to reveal the full picture — all the dominoes fallen,
forming a pattern or image that represents the complete solution.

TAG (40-45s)
Logo, tagline. The final domino.
```

## Structure 5: The Split Screen (30-45s)

Direct comparison. Old way vs new way. With vs without.

```
ESTABLISH BOTH WORLDS (0-5s)
Split the frame. Two versions of the same starting point.
Identical beginnings — the divergence hasn't happened yet.

THE DIVERGENCE (5-25s)
Left side: the old way. Chaos builds. Steps multiply. Time drags.
Right side: the new way. Smooth, fast, elegant.
The contrast should be DRAMATIC, not subtle.

THE CONVERGENCE (25-35s)
The old-way side cracks, breaks apart, or gets absorbed.
The new-way side expands to fill the full frame.
Victory for simplicity.

RESOLVE (35-45s)
Full-frame payoff. Product name. CTA.
```

## Structure 6: The Zoom (20-40s)

Start macro, go micro (or vice versa). Great for showing scale or detail.

```
THE WIDE SHOT (0-5s)
Show the big picture — an organization, a system, a workflow.
Everything looks fine from up here.

THE ZOOM IN (5-15s)
Push into the details. Reveal the hidden complexity,
the manual work, the friction that lives beneath the surface.

THE FIX (15-25s)
At the micro level, the product intervenes.
A surgical, precise transformation.

THE ZOOM OUT (25-35s)
Pull back out. The big picture has changed.
What looked fine before now looks *great*.
The improvement is visible at every scale.

CLOSE (35-40s)
Logo, CTA.
```

## Pacing Guidelines

| Duration | Cuts/Transitions | Beat Frequency | Best For |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|----------|
| 15s | 4-6 | Every 2-3s | Social ads, teasers |
| 30s | 6-10 | Every 3-4s | Feature launches, product highlights |
| 45s | 8-14 | Every 3-5s | Explainers, campaign heroes |
| 60s | 10-18 | Every 3-5s | Product stories, brand moments |
| 90s | 15-25 | Every 4-6s | Brand films, keynote openers |

## Music & Pacing Sync

- **Build beats:** Music swells should align with problem escalation
- **The drop:** The biggest musical moment = the solution reveal
- **Breath beats:** Quiet moments in music = emotional processing time for the audience
- **End tag:** Music resolves with the logo — finality, not fade-out

# Prompt patterns

Fill the brackets. `<STYLE LOCK>` means the base sentence from SKILL.md, pasted
word for word. For pictures with no people use the surfaces version:

```text
monochrome hand-drawn ink illustration style with wobbly brush-pen outlines of
uneven line weight, flat solid black fills, unshaded paper-white surfaces, pure
white background, no shading, no gradients, no grey tones, no colour
```

Every pattern below also needs the HAND-DRAWN STROKE block from SKILL.md as its
own paragraph, placed after the character anchor or after the subject. It is
left out of the templates here only to keep them short.

In character anchors, write faces the simple way: "two small dot eyes with
[brows], long simple single-line nose, small curved closed smile".

## Character anchor

```text
CHARACTER ANCHOR:
[name], [who and age range], [face shape and jaw], [eye shape, brows],
[nose], [mouth], [one or two marks or accessories], [hair as a solid black
shape: length, parting, how it is tied], [build, posture, oversized
expressive hands], <STYLE LOCK>
```

Leave clothing out of the anchor unless it never changes. Put it in the shot.

## Person doing something

```text
SHOT VARIABLE:
[action with both hands accounted for], torso facing the viewer [straight on /
three-quarter], framed from the [thighs / waist] up, wearing [garment] over a
solid black [garment], [two or three props placed left and right],
[expression], a few short motion ticks [where], generous white space, no text,
no logos, no extra characters
```

## Person, seated or full figure

```text
SHOT VARIABLE:
[seated cross-legged on the floor / standing], [what each hand holds],
[expression], [two small props beside them], full figure visible,
three-quarter view, generous white space around the figure, [mood], no text,
no logos, no extra characters
```

## Hand with an object

```text
SUBJECT:
a single oversized expressive hand and forearm reaching in from the [upper
left / lower right], [holding what, how], the forearm has a solid black sleeve
[pushed up to the elbow / cuff], [the object in detail, with items poking out
if it is a bag], a few short motion ticks around [object], the [object] hangs
in empty white space, generous margins, no body, no face, no text, no
lettering, no labels, no logos

STYLE:
<STYLE LOCK, surfaces version, with "paper-white skin and surfaces">
```

## Note or list with writing

```text
SUBJECT:
a slightly tilted paper note with a torn-off top edge, and an oversized
expressive hand entering from the lower right holding a pencil, mid-writing;
the hand has a solid black sleeve cuff; the note contains exactly [N]
handwritten lines, one under the other, each starting with a small empty
hand-drawn checkbox, reading exactly "[word]", "[word]", "[word]" in loose
casual handwriting; the pencil tip touches the end of the word "[last word]";
nothing else on the page, generous white space, no other words, no numbers,
no logos, no extra objects
```

Keep it to a few short words. Always check the spelling at full size.

## Cards or tiles

```text
SUBJECT:
[N] illustrated cards fanned out and slightly overlapping, each tilted at a
different angle, each card a rounded rectangle with a thick outline showing
one [thing]: [item], [item], [item], [item]; one card has a solid black
background with its drawing in white for contrast; a few short motion ticks
around the fan; cards float on empty white space, generous margins, no hands,
no people, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos
```

## Counter or tabletop full of things

```text
SUBJECT:
a [kitchen counter / desk] seen straight on, crowded with [several clearly
different containers, each named and shaped differently]; [items poking out
of and sitting beside them, each named]; one container and a few items are
filled solid black for contrast; the surface is a single thick horizontal line
with a simple drawer front below; generous white space above, no people, no
hands, no text, no lettering, no labels, no logos
```

## Shelf or storage

```text
SUBJECT:
an open [three]-tier shelving unit seen straight on, neatly stocked with many
different items: [list, each a different shape and height]; some [jars, tins
and bottles] are filled solid black for contrast; all labels are blank shapes
with nothing written on them; the shelf frame is drawn with a thick outline;
generous white space around the unit, no people, no hands, no text, no
lettering, no numbers, no logos
```

## Single object or icon

```text
SUBJECT:
one [object] drawn large and centred, slightly tilted, thick outline, [one
part] filled solid black, two or three short motion ticks beside it, nothing
else in the picture, wide empty margins, no text, no logos
```

## Idea shown as a metaphor

```text
SUBJECT:
[a small full-length figure / a head in side profile filled solid black] with
[small outlined icons for the idea: list] floating around in a loose ring,
short ticks radiating outward, a single thin baseline under the figure, lots
of empty white space, no text, no lettering, no logos
```

## An emblem drawn into the picture

The default for logos. Describe the mark as shapes, never as a name or a
letter, and give it its own paragraph after the shot variable:

```text
EMBLEM:
exactly in the centre of the [apron bib / tote bag front / mug / sign], level
and horizontally centred, one small emblem drawn in the same brush-pen hand:
[outer shape and its proportions], filled solid black, with [inner shape in
white, described precisely: how many curves, where it starts and ends]; clear
white [surface] all around the emblem; nothing else on the [surface], no
pocket, no pattern
```

In the shot variable, face the surface straight on and add "nothing overlaps
the [surface]". Afterwards crop the emblem from the full-size image and compare
it with the real logo.

## A blank panel, for a logo that must be exact

Only when the mark is too detailed to draw or has to be pixel-true:

```text
the [apron bib / tote bag front / mug / sign] is a large flat completely blank
white panel with nothing on it (no pocket, no pattern, no lettering, no
emblem), facing the viewer straight on, [hair / hands / props] do not cross it
```

Then place the real vector on it in black, centred on the panel.

## Expression sheet

```text
SHOT VARIABLE:
expression sheet with six head-and-shoulder drawings of the same person in a
clean grid: neutral, happy, surprised, worried, determined, amused; same hair,
same accessories, same line weight in every panel, no panel borders, no
captions, no text
```

## Changing only the outfit or pose

Keep the anchor identical and write only the difference in the shot variable.
State what must not change: "same hair, same glasses, same face".

# Proven examples

These exact prompts produced the images shown to the owner on 26 November 2026
(Figma `generate_image`, model `gpt-image-2.5-flare`, 1024 × 1024). Start from
the closest one and change as little as possible.

Only the cook below was made with the HAND-DRAWN STROKE block. The other
prompts predate it: when you reuse one, add the stroke block (see SKILL.md)
and "drawn simply with few lines" so the result matches the cook.

Came out as asked, except she holds the laptop up rather than resting it on
her knee.

## Meera, the cook, with M's emblem on her apron (the current standard)

This is the version made after the owner's corrections, and the first to carry
the HAND-DRAWN STROKE block. Use it as the model for every new person.

```text
CHARACTER ANCHOR:
Meera, Indian woman in her 30s, oval face with a soft jaw, two small dot eyes with short arched brows, long simple single-line nose with a tiny nose stud, small curved closed smile, a small round bindi between the brows, solid black hair with a centre parting pulled back into a low bun, small jhumka earrings, a few thin bangles on each wrist, medium build with relaxed upright posture and oversized expressive hands, monochrome hand-drawn ink illustration style with wobbly brush-pen outlines of uneven line weight, flat solid black fills, unshaded paper-white skin, pure white background, no shading, no gradients, no colour

HAND-DRAWN STROKE:
drawn quickly and loosely by hand with a brush pen: calligraphic outlines that swell and taper, slightly rough dry-brush edges on the thick strokes, a few lines left open, visibly imperfect and human, very simple cartoon face, minimal interior detail, no fine hair strands, no cross-hatching, no tiny textures

SHOT VARIABLE:
standing behind a kitchen counter cooking, torso facing the viewer straight on and centred in the picture, framed from the thighs up, stirring a steaming kadai (a deep round-bottomed Indian cooking pan with two small loop handles) on a small gas stove with a flat ladle in one hand, the other hand holding a loop handle with a folded cloth, beside the stove a round masala dabba spice box and a round rolling board with a rolling pin and two rotis, all drawn simply with few lines, wearing a plain white bib apron over a solid black kurta with elbow-length sleeves, apron strings tied at the waist, nothing overlaps the apron bib

EMBLEM:
exactly in the centre of the apron bib, level and horizontally centred, one small emblem drawn in the same brush-pen hand: a horizontal pill-shaped capsule badge about twice as wide as it is tall, filled solid black, with a single smooth white wavy line running across its middle that has exactly two rounded humps like a soft lowercase handwritten m; clear white apron all around the emblem; nothing else on the apron, no pocket, no pattern

cheerful focused expression, a few steam curls and short motion ticks above the kadai, generous white space, no text, no lettering, no other logos, no extra characters
```

The emblem came back centred, black, with the right proportions and the right
two-hump wave. The file had a transparent background and needed a white
backing before delivery.

### What was rejected on the way here

- **First cook (Odile in an apron):** the owner wanted the person to look
  Indian.
- **Second cook (Meera with a braid, real vector mark pasted on, in purple and
  in black):** the braid crossed the apron bib so the mark sat off-centre; the
  owner did not want any colour; and the drawing was too tidy and detailed
  (almond eyes with lashes, fine hair strands), prompting the request for
  Notion's hand-drawn texture and stroke.
